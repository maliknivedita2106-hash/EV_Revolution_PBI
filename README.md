# India's EV Revolution | Power BI Dashboard

An interactive Power BI project analysing India's Electric Vehicle (EV) adoption trends, state-wise registration patterns, and charging infrastructure distribution.

## Project Overview

India's electric vehicle market is evolving rapidly as the country moves towards sustainable mobility. This project uses data visualisation and business intelligence techniques to explore EV adoption across different states and analyse the availability of charging infrastructure.

The dashboard provides insights into EV registration trends from 2020 to 2023 and examines charging infrastructure data as of 1 January 2025.

## Business Objectives

- Analyse the growth of EV registrations across India.
- Identify states and union territories with higher EV adoption.
- Evaluate year-over-year EV registration growth.
- Examine the distribution of FAME-II charging stations and OMC stations.
- Identify potential gaps between EV adoption and charging infrastructure.
